  curl -H "Accept: text/html" http://localhost:8080/mobile/inbox  -> 404 {"errorCode":100001,"errorMessage":"api not found"}
  curl -H "Accept: text/html" http://localhost:8080/mobile/login  -> 404 (mismo JSON)
  curl -H "Accept: text/html" http://localhost:8080/login         -> 404 (mismo JSON)

ruta a ApiNotFound (JSON). Recargar la página en /mobile/inbox o abrir un enlace profundo falla.
